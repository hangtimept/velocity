Setup

1. Marker

Attach a bright solid color marker (recommended: neon green or orange tape/sticker) to the end of the barbell sleeve. Size 4–8 cm diameter works best.

2. Camera Position

Place your phone on a tripod or stable surface so the camera views the side of the bar. The full range of motion of the marker must stay in frame. Keep the phone parallel to the bar path (vertical for most lifts).
